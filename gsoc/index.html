How the ranking works

Each merged PR earns 10 points. If closed-without-merging PRs outnumber half your merged ones, each extra costs 2 points, and open PRs beyond your merged count cost 1 point each. Seasons run from September 1 to September 1. Merged PRs from repositories folded into the monorepo are included even though GitHub search no longer lists them, and PRs closed when the Django site retired on November 1, 2026 don't count against anyone.

Questions

What is Google Summer of Code?

A global program where new contributors work on open-source projects with mentors from participating organizations, and receive a stipend from Google.

Do I need prior experience?

Basic programming knowledge helps, but we welcome beginners who are eager to learn. Start with small fixes to understand our workflow.

Which project should I contribute to?

Most work happens in learn/, our live platform, but every folder in the monorepo is fair game. Pick the area your proposal will touch.

Why five merged PRs?

It shows you can work with our codebase and review process, which is the best predictor of a successful summer. Quality matters more than count.

Can I propose my own idea?

Yes. A focused proposal grounded in our code is just as strong as one from the list. Run it past a mentor in Discussions first.

Where do I ask questions?

Open a thread in GitHub Discussions, comment on an issue, or join our Slack community.
