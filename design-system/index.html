Documentation

Usage guide and component reference below, and in design-system/

01

The A1 mark

Two panels at a 1:2 slope: a white front panel carrying the A, a black back panel carrying the 1. The black panel always has a white edge and the white panel a black edge, so the mark reads on any background. Pair it with the ALPHA ONE LABS wordmark in Barlow Condensed 900.

02

White, black and brass

A restrained foundation: plain white and plain black do the work, brass gold is the signature accent for emphasis, labels, calls to action and circuits. Never gold on white for running text. Click a swatch to copy its token.

05

Circuit art

Drawn like a real board: traces run straight or at 45°, buses keep a fixed pitch, and every trace ends in a via or a pad. Use them for hero framing, dividers, corners and the footer, never over text. All use currentColor; set color: var(--a1-accent-fill).

06

The mascot

Curiosity, intelligence and teaching. Use him in the homepage hero, teacher recruitment, campaigns and onboarding, never in every section.

Treatment

  • Show portraits in a cropped rectangular panel with object-fit: cover.
  • Keep the eye line and glasses visible; never crop at the eyebrows.
  • They are not transparent cutouts. Don't layer them as one.
  • Pair the noir version with true white, true black, grays and gold.
  • No heavy gradients or glows around him.
  • Meaningful placement: describe him in alt text; decorative: empty alt.
07

Use it

Add these to any page in the monorepo. A new folder with a wrangler.toml deploys to <folder>.alphaonelabs.com on its own.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://design.alphaonelabs.com/brand.css">
<script src="https://design.alphaonelabs.com/chrome.js" defer></script>

<a1-header site="Your site" sticky></a1-header>   <!-- add compact for app-like pages -->
<main style="background: var(--a1-bg); color: var(--a1-text)">…</main>
<a1-footer source="your-folder"></a1-footer>

Light and dark

Light is the base; dark follows the system. The header toggle sets data-a1-theme on <html>, remembers it across every alphaonelabs.com site and fires a1-themechange.

Accessible

AA contrast in both themes, 16px minimum body, 3px gold focus rings, keyboard menus, reduced motion.

Edit it

Everything lives in design-system/. Change a token and every site follows.
