01 · Logo

The A1 mark

Two panels at a 1:2 slope: a white front panel carrying the A, a black back panel carrying the 1. The black panel always has a white edge and the white panel a black edge, so the mark reads on any background.

Clear space

Keep at least the width of the "1" stem clear on every side. Minimum size: 24px tall on screen, 10mm in print.

Source

Paths come straight from the original Illustrator artwork (alphaonelabs-shirt-art.eps, 2009). Pure black #000 and white only.

02 · Color

Black, white and solder gold

Taken from the makerspace poster. Black and white do the work; gold is for emphasis: one accent per view, not decoration everywhere. Click a swatch to copy its token.

Semantic tokens

Build with these, not raw colors. They flip automatically between dark and light.

--a1-bg  --a1-surface  --a1-surface-raised
--a1-border  --a1-text  --a1-text-muted
--a1-accent  --a1-accent-hover  --a1-on-accent
--a1-focus

Contrast

Solder gold on black is 9.4:1, so it's safe for any text. On white it's decorative only: text, links and buttons use gold ink (5.4:1) or black, which the light theme does for you.

06 · Use it

Two lines and you're on brand

Add these to any page in the monorepo. New folders with a wrangler.toml deploy to <folder>.alphaonelabs.com on their own.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://design.alphaonelabs.com/brand.css">
<script src="https://design.alphaonelabs.com/chrome.js" defer></script>

<a1-header site="Your site" sticky>
  <a slot="actions" class="a1-btn a1-btn--primary" href="/start">Start</a>  <!-- optional -->
</a1-header>

<main style="background: var(--a1-bg); color: var(--a1-text)">…</main>

<a1-footer source="your-folder"></a1-footer>

Light and dark

Pages follow the system theme. The header's toggle sets data-a1-theme on <html>, remembers it across every alphaonelabs.com site, and fires a1-themechange.

Accessible by default

AA contrast in both themes, visible focus rings, a keyboard-friendly site menu and reduced-motion support.

Edit it

Everything here lives in design-system/. Change a token and every site picks it up.
